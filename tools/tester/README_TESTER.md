# Abandon Cities spike test (about 15 min)

Thanks for helping! The game log records almost everything. You only need to do the clicks, and answer the ✏ questions.
Use new games only. Never turn the test mod on in a game you care about.

## Install

1. Unzip this package to a normal folder (for example your Desktop). Quit Civ VI.
2. Double-click **INSTALL.bat**. Press Enter if the guessed Civ VI folders look right; otherwise paste the right path.
   (If Windows shows "protected your PC": More info → Run anyway.)
3. Start Civ VI → Additional Content: turn on **Abandon Cities Spike Tools** and Gathering Storm. Turn every other mod off.

## Part 1: single player

New game: Pangaea, Standard, 3 AI, Prince, barbarians on. Found your capital. Open the test panel with **Ctrl+Shift+D**.

1. Click **S0 Setup**, **P Probes**, **S2a To 8**, **S2b Cut**, **S3 Step**.
2. Click **S4 Run**. Attack the enemy warrior next to city S4 with your new warrior. Click **S4 Read**.
3. Click **S6 Attach**. Open your capital, click the new **S6** button, go to the next city, close the city panel.
   ✏ Is the S6 button lined up with the other city buttons, and does it hide when the panel closes?
4. Click **S5 Self**.
   ✏ Do two test notifications appear on the right? Does clicking one move the camera to city S4?
5. End turn → **S3 Step** → end turn → **S3 Step** → end turn.
6. Click **S1 Run**, then **S1 Check**.
   ✏ Did the game freeze or crash? Does the end-turn button show a blocker such as "consider razing"?
7. Click **S1 Arm T**, end turn, click **S1 Check**.
8. Quit to the desktop (not just the main menu, or the log is incomplete).

If the game crashes at step 6: take a photo of the panel first. Then restart, start a new game, found the capital, click **S0 Setup**, and click **S1 One** three times (restart after any crash).

## Part 2: hotseat

1. New hotseat game: Large map, 3 human players and 1 AI. Same mods.
2. As player 1, found the capital, then click **S5 HS Setup**, **S5 HS Send**, **S1 HS Arm**.
3. End turns through players 2 and 3 and back to player 1.
   ✏ Does player 2 see the test notifications? Does player 3 see none?
4. Quit to the desktop, then double-click **COLLECT_LOGS.bat**. It puts a zip on your Desktop.

## Send back

The zip from your Desktop, your ✏ answers, and a photo of anything that looked wrong.
To remove the mod afterwards, double-click **UNINSTALL.bat**.
